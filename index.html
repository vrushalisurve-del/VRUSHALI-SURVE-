About

A researcher shaped by the classroom

Mathematics faculty and researcher with 10+ years of teaching experience across diploma and engineering education. Currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Mathematics at Parul University, where her work sits at the intersection of inventory modelling, deteriorating and perishable products, sustainable supply chains, and carbon mitigation.

Alongside research and publication, she is regularly entrusted with paper setting and examination duties for GTU and Parul University, and takes an active part in faculty development programmes, workshops and academic coordination.
